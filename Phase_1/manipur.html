Manipur: The Jewel of India

History & Culture

Described by Lord Irwin as the "Switzerland of India," Manipur is a land of undulating hills surrounding a football-shaped valley. It is the soul of the Meitei culture and home to colorful hill tribes like the Nagas and Kukis. Its history dates back to 33 AD under the deity king Pakhangba.

Manipur is the birthplace of the graceful Ras Leela dance, one of India's classical dance forms. The state represents a unique blend of ancient Sanamahism (worship of nature deities) and Vaishnavism. It is a land where martial arts (Thang-Ta) and artistic delicacy coexist in perfect harmony.

Interesting Facts

Some Interesting Facts about Manipur:

  • Birthplace of Polo: Modern Polo (Sagol Kangjei) originated here. The British learned it from Manipuri kings in the 19th century.
  • Floating National Park: The Keibul Lamjao National Park is the only floating national park in the world, located on Loktak Lake.
  • Women's Market: Ima Keithel in Imphal is the world's largest market run exclusively by women (over 3,000 mothers run the stalls).

Must-Try Local Flavors - Cuisine

  • Eromba: A spicy mash of boiled vegetables and fermented fish (Ngari), garnished with herbs.
  • Chak-hao Kheer: A stunning purple pudding made from the indigenous aromatic Black Rice.
  • Kangshoi: A healthy vegetable stew cooked with dried fish and fresh herbs.

Destinations

Loktak Lake: The Floating Wonder

  • Vibe: Serene & Unique
  • Key Attraction: The Phumdis (floating biomass islands) and the endangered Sangai deer (the dancing deer).
  • Don't Miss: A boat ride to Sendra Island and staying in a floating homestay on the lake.

Imphal: The Historic Capital

  • Vibe: Bustling & Historic
  • Key Attraction: Kangla Fort, the ancient seat of Manipuri kings.
  • Don't Miss: Shopping at Ima Keithel and visiting the Shree Govindajee Temple.

Ukhrul: The Misty Hills

  • Vibe: Cool & Scenic
  • Key Attraction: Home of the Tangkhul Nagas and the rare Shirui Lily (state flower), which grows nowhere else in the world.
  • Don't Miss: Trekking up Shirui Kashong Peak and exploring the limestone Khangkhui Caves.

Moirang: The Freedom Hub

  • Vibe: Patriotic & Cultural
  • Key Attraction: The INA Museum, where the Indian Tricolor was hoisted for the first time on Indian soil by the INA in 1944.
  • Don't Miss: The Lai Haraoba festival (if visiting in May) celebrated at the Thangjing Temple.

Travel Information and Tips

How to reach

1. By Air
  • Bir Tikendrajit International Airport (Imphal): It is well connected with major cities like Delhi, Kolkata, Guwahati, and Bangalore. It is the most convenient way to enter the state.
  • Helicopter Service: Available from Imphal to remote areas like Jiribam, Tamenglong, and Moreh (subject to schedule).
2. By Rail
  • Jiribam Railway Station: Located on the Assam-Manipur border. However, it is about 220km from Imphal.
  • Dimapur Station (Nagaland): The most popular railhead (215km away). Travelers usually take a train to Dimapur and then a bus or taxi to Imphal.
  • Future Project: The massive Imphal Railway line (featuring the world's tallest pier bridge) is under construction.
3. By Road

The road to Manipur is an adventure in itself.

  • From Dimapur (NH2): This is the lifeline of Manipur. Buses and Wingers run daily. The journey takes 7-8 hours through scenic Naga hills.
  • From Guwahati: Overnight luxury buses are available, taking about 12-14 hours.
  • From Silchar: You can enter via the Jiribam route (NH37), though road conditions vary.

Itineraries

Choose Your Vibe

The Classic Valley Circuit

Best for: Families, history buffs, and leisure travelers.

Day 1: Arrival | Route: Imphal City

  • Activity: Land in Imphal. Visit the Kangla Fort and the Govindajee Temple for evening aarti.
  • Stay: Imphal (Classic Grande or City Hotel).

Day 2: Mothers & Warriors | Route: Imphal Local

  • Activity: Morning at Ima Keithel (Mother’s Market). Visit the Imphal War Cemetery (WWII) and the State Museum.
  • Stay: Imphal.

Day 3: The Floating Lake | Route: Imphal ➔ Moirang ➔ Loktak

  • Activity: Drive to Moirang (45km). Visit the INA Museum. Proceed to Loktak Lake and take a boat to Sendra Island.
  • Stay: Sendra Cottages or a Floating Homestay on the Phumdis.

Day 4: Dancing Deer | Route: Keibul Lamjao

  • Activity: Early morning visit to Keibul Lamjao National Park. Try to spot the Sangai Deer from the viewpoint.
  • Stay: Imphal (Return by evening).

Day 5: Departure

  • Activity: Buy some Black Rice and local handloom items before your flight.
  • Stay: Departure.

The Hills & Nature Circuit

Best for: Trekkers, nature lovers, and explorers.

Day 1: Arrival | Route: Imphal

  • Activity: Arrival in Imphal. Acclimatize and enjoy a traditional Manipuri Thali.
  • Stay: Imphal.

Day 2: Up the Hills | Route: Imphal ➔ Ukhrul

  • Activity: A scenic 3-hour drive to Ukhrul (84km). Watch the landscape change from paddy fields to pine forests.
  • Stay: Ukhrul (Homestay or Hotel 25 Degree).

Day 3: The Lily Trek | Route: Shirui Peak

  • Activity: Trek to Shirui Kashong Peak to see the Shirui Lily (blooms May-June). Enjoy the panoramic views of the blue hills.
  • Stay: Ukhrul.

Day 4: Cave Exploration | Route: Khangkhui Caves

  • Activity: Visit the ancient limestone Khangkhui Mangsor Caves, used as a shelter during WWII. Explore Nungbi village famous for black pottery.
  • Stay: Ukhrul.

Day 5: Return to Valley | Route: Ukhrul ➔ Andro ➔ Imphal

  • Activity: Drive back towards Imphal. Stop at Andro Cultural Village to see the fire that has been burning for centuries.
  • Stay: Imphal.

Day 6: Departure

  • Activity: Fly out from Imphal with memories of the misty hills.
  • Stay: Departure.

Essential Travel Tips for Manipur

Important things to know before you pack your bags.

1. Inner Line Permit (ILP) is Mandatory

Manipur has implemented the ILP system. All non-native visitors (Indian citizens) must obtain a permit.

  • Where to get it: You can apply online via the official Manipur ILP portal or get it upon arrival at Imphal airport and entry check-posts (Mao/Jiribam).
  • Cost: Nominal fee for a temporary pass (usually valid for 15 days).

2. Sunrise and Sunset

Manipur is in the far east. The sun rises as early as 4:30 AM and sets by 4:30 PM in winter. Plan your days early. Most markets close by 6:00 PM or 7:00 PM.

3. Security Checks

While tourism is growing, there is heavy security presence in certain areas due to its border location. Always carry your original ID (Aadhaar/Voter ID) and permit. Cooperate politely at Assam Rifles or Police check-posts.

4. Dress Code

Manipur is socially conservative. When visiting temples like Govindajee, modest dress is required. For the Kangla Fort, walking shoes are recommended as the complex is huge.

5. Food Habits

Manipuri cuisine uses a lot of fermented fish (Ngari) and bamboo shoot. If you are vegetarian, specifically ask for "Veg-without-fish" or stick to the Vaishnavite Hindu hotels which serve pure vegetarian food.

6. Connectivity

Internet shutdowns can occasionally happen during times of unrest. Carry some cash and keep offline copies of your tickets and permits. Airtel and Jio generally have good 4G coverage in the valley and district headquarters.
